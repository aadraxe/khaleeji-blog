Read More: Articles, Organizations and Links

Compiled by Aarav, Kartheek and Tanish

Academic article

Martín, E. (2021). Nation building and social change in the United Arab Emirates through the invention of Emirati cuisine. The Sociological Review, 69(3). doi.org/10.1177/00380261211009090

Martín studied how chefs and cooks recognized as experts on Emirati food talk about it. She found Emirati cuisine described in three overlapping stages, traditional, modern and fusion, each matching a moment in the country’s development. She also shows that food draws lines of identity: who gets to share “authentic” flavors at the table, and who may reproduce them in restaurants.

Why it matters for us: The UAE only became a country in 1971, so “Emirati cuisine” as a national idea is new. The article explains why the dishes on our Food and Culture page are being actively protected and promoted.

Questions to think about

  • Can a national cuisine be invented, or does it have to grow on its own?
  • If a cook from another country prepares a traditional Emirati dish, is it still authentic?
  • Is fusion food a threat to tradition, or a way to keep it alive?

  • Qatar food history (Seasoned Traveller): Bedouin and coastal diets, trade routes and the oil era.
  • The Middle East’s smallest state offers some of the region’s biggest flavours (National Geographic): Bahrain’s food and its trade influences.
  • Gahwa (Abu Dhabi Department of Culture and Tourism): Arabic coffee and its etiquette.

  • What is fjiri? (The National, 2022): the music of Bahrain’s pearl divers.

Nonprofit organizations

Hospitality and sharing food are at the center of Khaleeji culture. These organizations carry that value into fighting hunger, in the Gulf, in Yemen and here in Tampa.

  • UAE Food Bank: launched in 2017 to collect surplus food and share it with people in need, with a goal of zero food waste (Gulf Business).
  • World Food Programme in Yemen: the UN’s food assistance agency, working in Yemen, the home of mandi and of the restaurant we visited.
  • Feeding Tampa Bay: the food bank serving our own community in Tampa.
