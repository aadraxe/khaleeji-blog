Tasting the Gulf: Our Presentation

By Tanish

Our class presentation asked one question: beyond the skylines, what do Khaleeji kitchens say about who people are? We used food as a lens on the region’s Bedouin roots, its coastal heritage, and an identity that is changing fast. This page is a quick guide to how the presentation was organized, with links to the blog post that goes deeper on each part.
