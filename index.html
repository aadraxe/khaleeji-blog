How Food Tells a Story of Culture, History, and Identity

Exploring the Khaleeji region, one plate at a time

Beyond the skylines of Dubai: what coffee, sweets and shared platters of rice say about the people of the Arabian Gulf.

Welcome

We are three USF Honors students in IDH 4200, Geographical Perspectives. Our group explored the Khaleeji region of the Arabian Gulf through its food.

Most people picture the Gulf as skyscrapers and luxury. We wanted to look past that, to the Bedouin and seafaring roots, the hospitality and the traditions families keep alive as their cities change. Along the way we researched the region, visited a Yemeni restaurant in Tampa, interviewed a restaurant staff member from the Khaleeji region, and presented what we learned to our class.
